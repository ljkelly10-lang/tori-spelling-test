Bible Verse Practice 📖

2 Timothy 3:16–17
Practice it a few different ways until the words begin to feel automatic.
Test: Friday, August 28

Read & Listen

Read along while the phone says the verse. Then try saying it with the voice.

“All Scripture is inspired by God, and is profitable for teaching, for rebuking, for correcting, for training in righteousness, so that the man of God may be complete, equipped for every good work.”
Memory trick: Notice the pattern: teaching → rebuking → correcting → training. Those four action words are the middle of the verse.

Learn It in Chunks

Say each chunk out loud three times. Then hide it and try to say it without looking.

Fill in the Missing Words

Start with Easy. When that feels comfortable, switch to Hard.

First-Letter Challenge

Each letter is the first letter of a word. Use the letters to help you say the whole verse.

“All Scripture is inspired by God, and is profitable for teaching, for rebuking, for correcting, for training in righteousness, so that the man of God may be complete, equipped for every good work.”

Put the Verse in Order

Tap the chunks in the correct order. If you make a mistake, tap a chosen chunk to send it back.

Try It From Memory

Type the verse without looking. Capitalization and punctuation do not count — the words do.
