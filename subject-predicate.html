Quick Study Rules

Complete subject = who/what the sentence is about + all describing words.
Complete predicate = what the subject does/is + all the words that go with the verb.
Simple subject = the main noun or pronoun.
Simple predicate = the main verb.
Best trick: Find the verb first. Then ask, “Who or what did that?”
Compound sentence: two complete thoughts joined with a comma + conjunction such as and, but, or.
